# ARBITEX Playtest Survey Questions

For use in a Google Form. Sectioned to match Google Forms' section-break feature. Suggested answer types are noted in brackets.

## Section A: Background

1. Age range [Multiple choice: Under 18 / 18-24 / 25-34 / 35+]
2. How often do you play video games? [Multiple choice: Rarely / A few times a month / A few times a week / Daily]
3. How familiar are you with survival horror games? [Linear scale 1-5, 1 = Never played one, 5 = Very familiar]
4. Do you have a background in computing/CS? [Multiple choice: Yes, studying/working in CS / Some exposure / No background]

## Section B: Overall Experience

5. Overall, how much did you enjoy playing ARBITEX? [Linear scale 1-5]
6. How would you rate the pacing of the explore, combat, and puzzle loop? [Linear scale 1-5, 1 = Poorly paced, 5 = Well paced]
7. Which part of the game did you enjoy most? [Multiple choice: Exploration / Combat / Puzzles / Story and atmosphere]
8. Which part of the game did you enjoy least? [Multiple choice: Exploration / Combat / Puzzles / Story and atmosphere]
9. How immersive or atmospheric did the game feel? [Linear scale 1-5]

## Section C: Combat

10. How would you rate the difficulty of the combat encounters? [Linear scale 1-5, 1 = Too easy, 5 = Too hard]
11. Did the combat difficulty feel appropriate to your skill level? [Linear scale 1-5]
12. Did you notice the enemy difficulty change as you played (e.g. enemies getting tougher or easier over time)? [Multiple choice: Yes, clearly / Maybe, not sure / No]
13. How did the shooting and weapon handling feel? [Linear scale 1-5, 1 = Clunky, 5 = Smooth]
14. Was there anything about combat that felt frustrating or unfair? [Paragraph, open text]

## Section D: Educational Puzzles

15. How would you rate the difficulty of the puzzles? [Linear scale 1-5, 1 = Too easy, 5 = Too hard]
16. Did the puzzle instructions and UI feel clear? [Linear scale 1-5]
17. Did you notice the puzzle difficulty adjusting based on how well you were doing? [Multiple choice: Yes, clearly / Maybe, not sure / No]
18. Did any puzzle help you recall or reinforce a computing concept you already knew, or teach you something new? [Multiple choice: Yes, taught me something new / Reinforced something I knew / Neither]
19. Which puzzle(s) did you find most memorable or enjoyable, and why? [Paragraph, open text]

## Section E: Adaptive Difficulty (overall)

20. Overall, did the game's difficulty feel like it adapted to how you were playing? [Linear scale 1-5, 1 = Felt fixed/static, 5 = Clearly adaptive]
21. When the difficulty changed (if you noticed it), did it feel natural or jarring? [Multiple choice: Natural / Jarring / Did not notice a change / Not sure]
22. Would you prefer a game that adjusts its difficulty to you, or one with a fixed difficulty you choose yourself? [Multiple choice: Adaptive / Fixed, chosen by me / No preference]

## Section F: Technical / UX

23. Did you encounter any bugs or glitches? If so, please describe them. [Paragraph, open text]
24. How clear was the on-screen UI (prompts, HUD, hints)? [Linear scale 1-5]
25. Were the controls easy to learn? [Linear scale 1-5]

## Section G: Wrap-up

26. What was the single most enjoyable moment of your playthrough? [Paragraph, open text]
27. What was the single most frustrating moment of your playthrough? [Paragraph, open text]
28. What is one thing you would change or add to the game? [Paragraph, open text]
29. How likely are you to recommend this game to a friend? [Linear scale 1-5, 1 = Not at all likely, 5 = Very likely]

---

Notes for setup:
- Sections C and D map to the two DDA systems (combat AI, puzzle AI) tracked separately in the project, so keeping them split lets you compare perception of each system.
- Questions 12, 17, 20, and 21 are the core DDA-perception questions; if you need to shorten the survey, keep these four plus the overall enjoyment and difficulty ratings.
- Consider adding a required "Which build/version did you play?" question if you run this across multiple playtest sessions with different builds, since answers won't be comparable otherwise.
